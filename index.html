Privacy Policy

Sonomètre (Sound Meter: dB & Spectrum) · last updated October 23, 2026

1. Summary

Sonomètre (“the app”, listed on Google Play as Sound Meter: dB & Spectrum) is a sound level meter published by Cool App Helpdesk. It reads the microphone to show noise levels, frequency bands and a spectrum, and lets you save measurement sessions and export them as PDF or CSV files.

  • Audio is never recorded, stored or sent. Microphone samples are processed in memory to compute levels and are discarded straight away. Only the computed levels (numbers in decibels) are kept, and only when you save a session.
  • Your sessions, notes and places are stored only on your device. They leave it only when you export or share a file yourself, or through your own Android backup.
  • Your location is read only when you tap “Use my location”, once, to tag a session. It is never sent to the developer.
  • The app contains Google’s advertising SDK (AdMob) for a banner on the History screen. Ads are switched off until September 1, 2027: until then the SDK is never started and collects nothing. From that date it may collect the data listed in section 2.
  • The only thing that ever reaches the developer is a feedback message you choose to send.

2. Data we collect

5. Network activity and third parties

Measuring, analysing, saving and exporting all work offline, on your device.

  • Google AdMob — included, but inactive until September 1, 2027: the SDK is not initialised before that date, so it makes no requests and reads no identifiers. From that date it loads a banner on the History screen and may collect the data in section 2. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • Address search and “Use my location” use your phone’s own Android location and geocoding services. Turning coordinates into an address, or an address into coordinates, is done by the geocoding service built into your phone (on most phones, Google’s), under that provider’s privacy policy. The app does not send your location to the developer or to any other service.
  • Feedback you send goes over HTTPS to the developer’s own feedback service, hosted on Google Cloud. It is used only to reply to you and improve the app, and is never sold or shared.
  • Links in a PDF report to OpenStreetMap open only if you or the reader tap them.

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs and no social or attribution SDKs. When you share an export through the system share sheet, the file goes to the app you choose; the developer does not see it.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Sessions and places stay on your device until you delete them in the app (History or Saved places), clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Sonomètre → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to answer and act on them. To have feedback you sent deleted, email the address below.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. Feedback is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
